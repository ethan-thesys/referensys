# Referensys for Windows

Records a procedure as you do it. Every click in any program (your pharmacy software, Chrome, Edge, anything) becomes a step with a screenshot, an orange ring where you clicked, and a written title such as *Click "Verify"*. Then you cover patient details, edit the wording and export a PDF.

Your recordings, screenshots and PDFs never leave your computer. The only thing Referensys connects to is GitHub, to check for new versions of itself.

## Install

1. Double-click **Referensys-Setup-1.0.1.exe**.
2. If Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. This appears because the app isn't from a registered publisher.
3. Referensys installs and opens. You'll find it on your desktop and in the Start menu.

## Updates

Referensys updates itself. Each time it opens (and every few hours while it's open), it checks for a newer version and downloads it in the background. When one is ready, a bar says **Update ready**:

- **Restart and update** installs it now. Save your work first.
- **Later** installs it the next time you close Referensys.

You never need to download Referensys again.

## Recording

1. Click **Record → My screen: every click**. Referensys hides and a small bar appears in the top-right corner.
2. Do the task in your pharmacy software. Every left click adds a step.
3. Use **Pause** on the bar for clicks you don't want recorded, then **Resume**. Click **Done** when finished.
4. Go through each step: drag **Redact** boxes over patient details, fix the wording and tick the PHI check.
5. Click **Export PDF** and choose where to save it.

The small bar doesn't appear in your screenshots.

## Good to know

- **Check titles for PHI.** Step titles come from the name of what you clicked. If you click a patient's name, that name can end up in the title. To stop using on-screen text in titles, untick **Use button and field names in step titles** in the Record menu.
- Text you type into fields is never used in titles.
- Some programs don't share button names with Windows. Those steps get a title like *Click in ProgramName* for you to edit.
- If your pharmacy software runs **as administrator**, Windows won't let Referensys see clicks inside it. Right-click Referensys and choose **Run as administrator** to record it.
- **Practice pharmacy** in the Record menu has fake patients for trying things out.
- **Save** keeps a project you can reopen later with **Open**. Saving makes redactions permanent and never stores the unredacted screenshots.
- To uninstall: **Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Referensys → Uninstall**.
