Referensys

Referensys turns a task into a step-by-step standard operating procedure (SOP). Each click becomes a step with a screenshot, a title and written instructions. Personal details are covered before anything is saved.

Referensys saves SOPs to the signed-in user's own Google Drive, in a folder it creates called "Referensys SOPs".
